B. Model Architecture & Multi-Task Learning
Upgrade from Random Forest to Gradient Boosting (LightGBM / CatBoost)

Audio features often exhibit asymmetric distributions and correlations. LightGBM and CatBoost:
Better separate non-linear decision boundaries for subtle speech pauses.
Provide native categorical handling and faster GPU/CPU training.
Can be tuned with Bayesian optimization (optuna) on learning_rate, num_leaves, and reg_alpha.

Cost-Sensitive Learning / Focal Loss for High Support

Misclassifying a child who needs high_support as low_support has a higher educational cost than the reverse.
Use class_weight="balanced_subsample" or a custom cost matrix that penalizes false negatives on high_support.
