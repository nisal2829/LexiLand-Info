Slide 2

The Challenge

Why an integrated, bilingual support platform is needed

LEXILAND • COMMON RESEARCH PROJECT 02

Many children experience reading-related difficulties, but the support available to them can be fragmented.

LATE IDENTIFICATION

Difficulties may go unnoticed until a learner falls behind.

LANGUAGE GAP

Sinhala-specific screening resources are limited; many tools focus on English.

SINGLE-SKILL TOOLS

Existing solutions often address one skill or one stage of support.

LIMITED FOLLOW-THROUGH

Screening is not always connected to personalised practice and progress tracking.

Slide 4

The Four Components

Each component explores a different skill area; together they build a broader support profile

LEXILAND • COMMON RESEARCH PROJECT 04

01

Working Memory

Rabbit guide

Looks at how learners hold and sequence numbers, words, pictures and instructions.

02

Phonological Awareness

Parrot guide

Explores sound awareness, letter–sound links and spelling-related skills in Sinhala and English.

03

Reading Processing

Zebra guide

Examines reading accuracy, response timing and gaze-related behaviour during reading.

04

Speech Processing

Lion guide

Uses reading-aloud recordings and speech analysis to explore pronunciation, decoding and fluency.

Slide 9

Responsible and Ethical Use

Designed to support learning—not replace specialist assessment

LEXILAND • COMMON RESEARCH PROJECT 09

• Results indicate areas where a learner may need support; they are not clinical diagnoses.

• Persistent difficulties should be discussed with qualified education or health professionals.

• Children’s profiles, recordings and gaze-related data require careful protection.

• Consent, data minimisation, access control and transparent data practices are important.

• Larger, more diverse studies are needed to validate results across grades and languages.
